Multiverse Showdown

Roblox Luau Roblox Studio In Development

About the project

Multiverse Showdown is a Roblox game that is currently in development. I contributed to the project as a programmer, focusing specifically on gameplay scripting.

My contribution

  • Dash System. The player activates the dash with Q. When Shift Lock is active and W is held, the player performs a long forward dash with an attack. With A, S, or D held under the same conditions, the player dashes in those directions without an attack. When Shift Lock is inactive, the dash direction can be changed.
  • Running System. I also scripted the game's running mechanic.

My role

Programmer / Luau Scripter. I worked on the project's scripting rather than art or 3D assets.
